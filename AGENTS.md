About my educational and work background:
I graduated from Purdue University after studying business management and finance but learned quickly at my first corporate job outside of school that I wanted to do technical work so I taught myself to program and began freelancing for friends and family first, then I moved on to fixing, building, and migrating websites for small businesses. When I had the opportunity to take my skills to the next level in the highly regulated healthcare industry I took the leap with Mindful Care and started by first designing and building them an entirely new web application that would not only replace their current Wordpress website, but could also integrate with their electronic health record (EHR) which is what they use for day-to-day clinical work and operations. The impact I made in my first 2 months was was so incredible that I was quickly given more responsibility and tasked with hiring their entire technical department which would go on to automate more of the receptive and tedious tasks of the care team, such as patient intake, scheduling, insurance verification, and payments. Over the course of my time their I discovered an incredibly expensive and underpowered problem which was patient text messaging. While the EHR they were using had built-in SMS features, they were only able to send messages, but couldn't do 2-way communication for back and forth conversations, and also they existing tools that offered HIPAA-compliant service were very expensive. This is when I decided to build my own SaaS service, Patient Glue, which was a low-cost SMS platform for healthcare practices that included automations for patient scheduling, rescheduling, waitlists, reminder, pre-appointment calls, and was fully integrated with your EHR so you could see patient appointments at a glance without switching between apps. I developed and designed all of the features myself with feedback from users and constant iteration. At the end of 2025 I shut the service down because I could no longer compete with larger competitors that were offering lower cost messaging when clients signed a long term contract and so after losing my biggest customer I decided to build an iOS app Spit Notes (https://getspitnotes.com) to scratch an itch I had for years as a songwriter, essentially combining your notes and voice memos app into one so that artists could keep their lyrics right next to their audio.
